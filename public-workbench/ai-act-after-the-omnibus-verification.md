Companion to [the brief](ai-act-after-the-omnibus.md). It has one row for each legal date, article number, institution fact and Anthropic fact in the brief, and one for each of the author's judgments, so that each can be signed off or struck before publication.

## How to read it

- **verified-primary**: the primary source was opened and says this. For the Anthropic and Google Cloud pages and the repository runs, that happened on 14 October 2026 (row 73: 15 October 2026). One row relies on the author's own source note, and says so.
- **mirror-checked**: the provision was read in the GitHub mirror `legalize-dev/legalize-eu`, file `eu/e0/32024R1689.md`: the consolidated version 02024R1689-20260727 at commit `24a04e3b` (the file is unchanged at the mirror's HEAD `860a4c42`) and, for comparisons, the 2024 text at commit `30bf0307`. The mirror reformats EUR-Lex texts, in English only, and its README states: "This reformatted corpus is not an official publication. Consolidated texts have no legal effect; consult the authentic Official Journal edition." It is not EUR-Lex. Treat these rows as unconfirmed until the author reads the Official Journal.
- **secondary-only**: no primary text could be opened. The claim rests on search extracts of the primary page or on law-firm and press commentary. Treat it as unconfirmed until the author checks the Official Journal or the Bundesgesetzblatt.
- **unverified**: no source was opened for it, or it is a legal reading or judgment for the author.
- **Quotation**: given only where the text was retrieved in this run, otherwise "not retrieved". A script checked each quotation from an Anthropic or Google Cloud page, and each quoted run output, against the text retrieved or produced on 14 October 2026 (row 73: the run output and the PyPI JSON of 15 October 2026), and each quotation from the mirror (rows 13 and 75 to 77, and its README) against the mirror version it is attributed to (commits `24a04e3b` and `30bf0307`). Model names are left out on purpose.
- **Author sign-off**: pending on every row.
- A claim that rests on two sources with different status (the cloud DORA terms) has one row for each.

Totals: 78 rows, of which 26 verified-primary, 4 mirror-checked, 35 secondary-only and 13 unverified.
