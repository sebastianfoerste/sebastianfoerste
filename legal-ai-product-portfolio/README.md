# Legal AI Product Portfolio

This portfolio map is designed for reviewers of Legal Engineer, Product Specialist, Legal AI and AI SaaS Legal Operations roles.

It explains how the public repositories fit together as a coherent proof of work portfolio rather than as isolated prototypes.

## Review in 10 Minutes

Start with these repositories:

| Repository | Review purpose |
|---|---|
| contract-review-eval-harness | Shows how legal AI outputs can be evaluated with expected answer coverage, citation checks and hallucination tracking |
| legal-ops-agent | Shows how legal work can be structured into intake, triage, reviewer routing and approval controlled export |

## What This Portfolio Demonstrates

1. Legal workflow decomposition
2. Human in the loop review design
3. Evaluation and quality control for legal AI
4. Source grounding and auditability
5. Product adoption thinking for law firms and in house legal teams
6. Regulatory classification and documentation workflows
7. Public safe use of synthetic examples

## Portfolio Thesis

Useful legal AI is not merely prompt engineering. The stronger product question is whether legal work can be made structured, reviewable, testable and accountable.

The repositories in this portfolio therefore focus on intake, triage, evaluation, review states, blocked exports, audit trails, adoption blockers and source aware regulatory workflows.
