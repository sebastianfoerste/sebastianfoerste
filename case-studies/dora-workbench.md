# DORA Third-Party Register and Resilience Workbench

Public architecture note for a private legal engineering prototype.

## Summary

A supervised workflow for DORA third-party ICT risk governance. The project structures provider records, criticality analysis, review states, evidence fields and follow-up actions for operational resilience work.

## Problem

DORA turns ICT outsourcing and resilience governance into a structured data and workflow problem. Legal advice alone is not enough. Regulated entities need registers, review steps, evidence trails, accountability and repeatable controls.

## Workflow

1. Record ICT provider and service information.
2. Map services to functions and business relevance.
3. Classify criticality and review priority.
4. Capture contractual, technical and operational evidence.
5. Route records through review states.
6. Track gaps, decisions and follow-up actions.
7. Maintain an audit-ready register.
8. Produce management-ready summaries from reviewed data.

## Design principles

* Structured register before narrative reporting.
* Human review for criticality and legal interpretation.
* Evidence trails for supervisory and internal governance use.
* Clear distinction between operational data, legal assessment and final decision.
* Workflow design that makes resilience governance repeatable rather than episodic.
